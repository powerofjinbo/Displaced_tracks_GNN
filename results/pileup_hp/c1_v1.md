# Gate C1: the TrackML-style digitiser against TrackML training hits (step H-P, truth only)

This note covers the digitiser of `docs/pileup_overlay_plan.md` section 5.2 and its gate C1 (section 10), run on
2026-10-14. No model was run, no TrackML test or part03 event was read, and the TrackML hits used are training events
only. Code: `src/hnl/overlay/digitise.py` and `src/hnl/overlay/c1.py`; job script `slurm/overlay/c1.sbatch`; tests
`tests/test_overlay_digitise.py`. Outputs: `c1_v1.json` (summaries, settings, hashes) and `c1_v1_bins.csv` (every bin
of every comparison). The scratch scripts behind the diagnostic files below are in
`/work/submit/jinboz1/hnl/pileup_design/hp_digitiser/`.

## What the digitiser does

Each crossing is moved onto the mid-plane of its TrackML module and stepped as a straight line through a slab. The
slab's half thickness is module_t / 2 in the barrel and module_t in the endcaps. Charge drifts to the readout side with
the GenericDetector Lorentz angle. Cells lie on TrackML's pitch grid over the module's bounding box. The hit sits at the
value-weighted mean of the cell centres plus the Lorentz shift.

Two changes from the prototype (`hit_format/analysis.py cmd_pydigi`) came out of TrackML's raw cells (throughput
dev events 21000-21002, `c1_raw_cells_v1.json`):

- **Strip positions.** TrackML puts a strip hit at the plain mean of its strip centres. All strip values are 1, and the
  hit lies within 0.34 um of that mean in volume 13, which is the CSV rounding. The prototype weighted the strips by
  path length.
- **Pixel cell values.** TrackML's pixel value is the path length times a factor that scatters from cell to cell. The
  ratio of TrackML's value to the digitiser's path in the same cell has a width of 0.100 for paths above 0.04 mm. The
  width is the same in barrel and endcaps, the median ratio is 1.000, and the factors of two cells of one hit are
  uncorrelated (correlation -0.01 in the barrel, -0.03 in the endcaps; `c1_cell_factor_v1.json`, 0.74 M cells). The
  digitiser draws one factor per pixel cell from N(1, 0.100). This alone produces TrackML's per-hit charge spread, so
  it replaces the per-hit charge factor of plan section 4.4. No per-hit factor should be applied on top.
  `digitise` still accepts a per-hit factor (`charge_factor`) for callers written to the plan. It warns when the
  factor is used alone and raises when the factor comes together with the per-cell factor.

Without the factor, the digitiser reproduces TrackML's cells one by one. Replayed on the truth of events 21000-21002,
98.1-99.6 % of hits per volume get exactly TrackML's cell set, and the mean spans agree to the third decimal
(`c1_cell_replay_v1.json`). The other hits differ mostly by one corner cell with a path of a few um.

| 9, pixel endcap +z | 17.23 / 9.42 | 17.23 / 9.27 | 17.24 / 9.42 | 16.14 / 9.47 | 4.1 / 2.0 |
| 13, short-strip barrel | 17.04 / - | 17.01 / - | 17.01 / - | 18.14 / - | 16.3 / - |

Source: `c1_v1.json`, key `overall` (40 calibration events). The gun column has no incidence matching.

- **Pixel endcaps.** Here the cause was the sample, not the digitiser. In TrackML, 94 % of pixel-endcap hits are one
  pixel wide in u (median |tan_u| = 0.006), so the u residual is the binary resolution of a 50 um pitch. The
  prototype's muon sample had 43 % of its 840 volume-7 hits at |tan_u| > 0.1, against 1 % in TrackML. Its mean span0
  was 2.24 against 1.09. Wide clusters with exact path values give residuals of a few um. On TrackML's own truth the
  digitiser gives 17.3 / 9.3 um, even without the cell factor.
- **Pixel barrel, v.** Here the cause is the missing cell factor. Barrel clusters are long in v (mean span1 6.5). With
  exact path values their centroid is good to 0.7 um, and the per-cell scatter widens it to TrackML's 3.9 um.
- **Strips.** The plain mean of the strip centres widens the residual slightly against the path-weighted mean of the
  prototype.

No resolution smear (plan section 4.4) is needed.

## Gate C1

The rules are those of section 10: bins with at least 200 hits on each side count; mean span0 and span1 within 10 %;
pixel charge x cos(incidence) within 5 %; MAD of the u residual (all volumes) and of the v residual (pixels) within
20 %; mean u bias within 1 um; effective thickness within 0.04 mm. Spans, MADs and biases are binned in the incidence
angle that drives them (tan_u, signed in the barrel, or |tan_v|), and the charge in |tan|.

**How the bins are compared.** Local incidence has two angles. The gun fills them differently from TrackML: its hits
in a given tan_u bin are more forward and more often displaced. The first evaluation used plain bins of the driving
angle only, and it failed 7 of 525 bins, all in the pixel barrel (`gun_unmatched` in `c1_v1.json`). We checked these
before changing anything (`c1_barrel_diag_v1.json`):

- **MAD(du) at |tan_u| = 0.8-2.4.** The gun's median |tan_v| there is 3.4, TrackML's 0.8. More cells per column
  average the cell factor down. In |tan_v| sub-bins the two agree, for example 2.40 against 2.38 um at |tan_v| =
  0.3-1. Reweighted to TrackML's |tan_v|, the gun gives 2.26 um against 2.24 um.
- **span1 at |tan_v| = 8-16.** TrackML's barrel hits stop near |tan_v| = 10, while the gun's reach 16. In sub-bins of
  one unit the means agree (23.50 against 23.53 at 8-9, 26.08 against 26.14 at 9-10).

The gate is therefore evaluated at matched incidence. In each bin the gun hits are reweighted to TrackML's joint
distribution of the binning angle (reference quartiles within the bin) and the other angle (the same fine bins), on
the cells both samples fill. The thresholds are unchanged. This change of the comparison was made after the first
result was seen, and both results are kept.

## Open points

- **Pixel barrel at large u-inclination.** The closest bin to the MAD threshold is the pixel barrel at tan_u = -2.4 to
  -1.6: the gun is 18.9 % narrower, from 249 hits. Even on TrackML's own hits, the replay is 16-18 % narrower at
  |tan_u| > 2.4 (bins of 215-237 hits). The straight-line model with the cell factor seems to miss a small effect of
  low-momentum, strongly inclined tracks. Only 0.2 % of pixel-barrel hits have |tan_u| > 1.6, and every such bin
  passes.
- **Emulation.** The pixel charge factor of plan section 4.4 is replaced by the per-cell factor. The emulation hash
  should include `CELL_SIGMA = 0.100` and the digitiser seed rule (`event_rng(seed, event)`).
- **For gate F1.** Fatras reported two particles of the C1 sample that failed with "Interpolation out of bounds" of the
  field map. Both are in event 733 (displaced vertices at r = 422 and 584 mm, |z| < 400 mm, p = 4.4 and 6.5 GeV). That
  is 2 of 100,000 particles (log of job 6819564).
