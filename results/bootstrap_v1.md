# Event-bootstrap uncertainties of the headline numbers (bootstrap_v1)

Supervisor feedback round 1, point 5 (`python -m hnl.eval.bootstrap_tables`; module docstring for the method). Validation numbers (val) and post-freeze descriptive summaries of the one-time test outputs (test; not claims: results/claims_test_v1.json stays as it is). Nothing is chosen here: every working point is read from the committed val files.

- **value**: mean of the seeds' numbers (the ranking tables' estimator); **seed sd**: sd of the seeds' numbers (ddof 1), the +- of the ranking tables; **boot sd**: event-bootstrap sd of the seed mean (events resampled with replacement, the same resampled events for every seed and model); **combined**: sqrt(boot sd^2 + seed sd^2 / n_seeds), the total sigma of hnl.eval.claims.
- Val: pooled HNL val (hnl10_100 + hnl15_200 val, 1,999 events, resampled by event index on 1,000 units) and ttbar val (1,000 events, ttbar-prompt column), B = 1000 (new tables, seed 20261005). Test: pooled HNL test sets (hnl_joint_test by event index + hnl15_100_external, 6,999 events) and ttbar_test (1,000 events) with the preregistered tables of the claims (B = 500, seed 12345).
- 68 % CP: Clopper-Pearson on the per-seed particle count (k = value x n per seed; paper Fig. 7 convention). 68 % bootstrap: 15.87 / 84.13 % percentiles of the replicates of the seed mean.
- Single-seed rows (DeZoort, ladder, previews): no seed term (as in claims.py), so their combined sigma is the bootstrap sd alone and understates the total.

- Labels: CC = connected components at the preregistered cut rule, CC* = at the best cut <= FR* chosen on val; MWS+M = MWS-layer + merging, WT+M = walkthrough + merging; Canto-L / -H / -B = the full-budget models at their rule-R6 builder (Canto-L: learned merging, selected by R8; Canto-H: WT+M; Canto-B: learned merging).
